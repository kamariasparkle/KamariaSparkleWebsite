Party Ideas

10 Birthday Party Ideas in Nairobi That Kids Will Love

Planning a child's birthday does not have to mean trying to organise everything at once. Start with the experience you want the children to have, then build the décor, entertainment and equipment around it.

1. Create a clear theme

Choose one simple theme and let it guide the colours, backdrop, balloons and cake area. A strong theme makes the whole setup feel intentional.

2. Add a Bubble House or castle

A dedicated play feature gives children something exciting to enjoy while also becoming part of the visual setup.

3. Create a photo area

A styled backdrop and balloon installation can give parents a beautiful place to capture the day's memories.

4. Think about the guest experience

Plan shade, seating, movement between activities and enough space for the equipment you hire.

5. Keep the details coordinated

Matching colours across the balloons, tables, cake area and signage can make even a simple celebration feel polished.

Event Planning

How to Plan a Memorable Event in Nairobi

A successful celebration usually starts with a clear plan. Before booking suppliers, decide what the event should feel like, who is attending and what matters most to you.

Start with the occasion and guest list

Your guest numbers and type of event will influence the venue, seating, entertainment, décor and equipment you need.

Choose the experience before the extras

Decide on the main experience first. For a children's party that might be a play feature; for an adult celebration it could be styling, music, food or an outdoor cinema.

Book important suppliers early

Popular dates can fill quickly. Confirm the venue and key equipment or styling requirements before leaving everything to the last minute.

Leave room for setup

Ask suppliers about delivery, setup and collection times so that the event space is ready before guests arrive.

Hire Guide

What to Consider Before Hiring Event Equipment

When comparing event hire options, price is only one part of the decision. The quality of the equipment and the service behind it can make a big difference to your event.

Check the condition and presentation

Clean, well-maintained equipment will fit much better into a carefully styled celebration.

Ask what is included

Confirm whether delivery, setup, collection, an attendant or supporting equipment is included in the package.

Consider the space

Measure the available area and check access before booking larger equipment such as castles, Bubble Houses, tents or cinema screens.

Confirm timing

Agree on delivery and setup times in advance so there are no surprises on the day.
